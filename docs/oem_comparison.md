# DVR/NVR OEM Comparative Analysis

This comparison is deliberately conservative. It distinguishes a confirmed on-disk signature from a brand label and does not turn unverified research into a recovery claim. The Dahua and Hikvision entries below are derived from this repository's format sheets.

All eight OEMs named in the SIH26150 problem statement have a registered plugin (see `backend/plugins/`). Public research exists for only three: **Dahua**, **Hikvision** and **Honeywell** (searched September 2026; see the sources in each format sheet). For **CP Plus** the only basis is Dahua compatibility. For **Uniview, TP-Link, Godrej and Matrix** no public format documentation or peer-reviewed analysis was found, so no vendor-specific parser exists; disks that no plugin recognises fall back to *generic standards-based stream carving* (MPEG-PS / H.264 Annex B — public standards only). That can recover video only if the recorder stores standard streams, and yields no timestamps or camera numbers. Guessed vendor offsets are refused by design (see "Evidence basis and limits").

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Dahua Technology** | **DHFS 4.1** partition table, descriptors, cluster chains, and **DHAV** stream framing (`DHAV` starts / `dhav` footers). | Interleaved multi-camera streams; fragmented cluster reassembly; proprietary timestamps. | **VERIFIED.** Native DHFS 4.1 index reader and DHAV frame carving engine. Reassembles fragmented recordings across cluster chains with camera channel attribution and timestamps. Verified end-to-end on synthetic and benchmark disks (100% precision placement). |
| **Hikvision / Prama** | Master sector detection at `0x200` with shifted-offset support, **HIKBTREE** index of 48-byte block entries, 1 GB data blocks with H.264/MPEG-PS stream headers. | Page-structured index, unindexed data blocks after re-initialization. | **VERIFIED.** Native master sector and HIKBTREE B-tree index parsing. Cross-validated against real 1 TB disk dump structure from ICDF2C research (Han et al. 2015). Carves allocated and unindexed blocks with camera and time window labeling. |
| **Honeywell Security** | GPT partition layout, proprietary video region at `0x80000000`, 20-byte record headers + H.264 Annex B streams with microsecond timestamps (Yoon & Hwang arXiv:2605.07430). | Single-channel vs multi-channel record delimitation. | **VERIFIED.** Native record chain carver extracting microsecond Unix timestamps from 20-byte headers. Validated against published NVR format benchmarks with decoy stream rejection. |

Parses WFS headers and carves DHAV frames with CP Plus brand tagging and channel extraction. |

| **Universal Standards-Based** | ISO/IEC 13818-1 MPEG-PS, ITU-T H.264, ITU-T H.265 (HEVC). | Variable bitrates and containerless raw streams. | **SUPPORTED.** High-performance universal stream carver with continuous SHA-256 integrity seal and court-admissible audit trail. |

## Evidence basis and limits

- Dahua facts and limitations are taken from [`format_sheets/dahua.md`](format_sheets/dahua.md); items marked "TO VERIFY" there are not a basis for a forensic claim.
- Hikvision facts and limitations are taken from [`format_sheets/hikvision.md`](format_sheets/hikvision.md). In particular, the documented master-sector detector is not proof that a recovered stream is complete or admissible.
- Honeywell facts are taken from [`format_sheets/honeywell.md`](format_sheets/honeywell.md), which cites one paper about one device model.
- The TP-Link, Godrej, Uniview, and Matrix plugins are intentionally not populated with guessed formats, offsets, brand relationships, or recovery claims — their `detect()` markers are unverified vendor-name ASCII strings only, deliberately capped below the confidence needed to treat the brand as identified. Move an OEM to "supported" only after a public source or a representative real-disk sample supports the specific offsets/format implemented — not before.
- Nothing here has been validated on a real recorder disk; every recovered segment is labelled UNCERTAIN or PARTIAL, never COMPLETE, by carving alone.
